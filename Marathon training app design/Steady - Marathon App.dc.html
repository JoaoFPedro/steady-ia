The two ideas, made concrete. Adaptive shows up as a "plan updated" strip that names what changed and why. Confidence shows up as a single readiness line the app never hides — on the home screen, in the plan, and on the race screen — so the answer to "will I finish?" is always one glance away.

System notes. Nocturne dark ground, accent used as line and glow only, outlined primaries, no saturated floods. Maps are placeholder routes — drop in real map tiles and photography when you have them.
